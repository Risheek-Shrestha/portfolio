I'm an MCA (AI/ML) student at the Yogananda School of AI, Computers and Data Sciences, Shoolini University (2025–27), based in India. My work sits at the seam between backend engineering and applied ML — building the services and the models that sit behind them.

Most of what I ship falls into one of three shapes: a REST API that authenticates, stores, and serves data; a full-stack app that wraps that API in something people actually use; or a trained model that turns a row of features into a prediction. I like projects with a clear input and a clear output — it's easier to know when they actually work.
